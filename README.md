🎬 Stream Flix OTT Analytics Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The Stream Flix OTT Analytics Dashboard is a Python-based data analytics project designed to analyze streaming platform performance, subscriber engagement, content performance, and business KPIs.

The project uses Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib to clean datasets, perform exploratory data analysis, calculate business metrics, and create a professional dashboard with 10 visualizations and 10 KPI cards.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean and validate streaming platform datasets.
- Analyze subscriber and viewing activity.
- Understand content distribution and genre performance.
- Analyze ratings and customer review sentiment.
- Calculate important business KPIs.
- Generate a professional OTT analytics dashboard.

🛠️ Technologies Used

Technology| Purpose
Python| Data analysis and programming
Pandas| Data cleaning and manipulation
NumPy| Numerical calculations
Matplotlib| Data visualization
Matplotlib Dates| Monthly trend visualization

📂 Dataset Description

The project uses six CSV datasets:

1. "subscribers.csv" – Subscriber information and subscription details.
2. "reviews.csv" – Customer reviews and sentiment.
3. "watch_history.csv" – Viewing activity and watch duration.
4. "titles.csv" – Movies, TV shows, genres, and content details.
5. "ratings.csv" – Content ratings.
6. "watchlist.csv" – Subscriber watchlist information.

🚀 Project Workflow

Phase 1: Data Cleaning & Validation

- Imported all six CSV datasets.
- Standardized column names.
- Checked dataset dimensions and data types.
- Identified missing values.
- Converted date and numeric columns.
- Converted Boolean columns.
- Detected negative values.
- Performed outlier detection using the IQR method.
- Checked referential integrity between subscriber and title IDs.

Phase 2: Dashboard & Data Visualization

Created 10 visualizations:

1. Monthly Viewing Volume
2. Monthly Watch Activity
3. Movies vs TV Shows
4. Top 10 Genres
5. Top 10 Countries
6. Device Usage
7. Ratings Distribution
8. Review Sentiment
9. Recent Release Years
10. Top 10 Watchlist

Phase 3: Business KPI Analysis

Calculated 10 important business KPIs:

KPI| Description
Total Watch Hours| Total streaming hours
Active Rate| Percentage of active subscribers
Churn Rate| Percentage of inactive subscribers
Completion Rate| Average content completion percentage
Monthly Revenue| Monthly recurring revenue from active subscribers
ARPU| Average Revenue Per Active Subscriber
Average Watch Time| Average watch hours per subscriber with viewing records
Watchlist Conversion| Percentage of watchlist entries marked as watched
Hit Concentration| Share of plays generated by the top 10% of titles
Original Share| Percentage of watch hours spent on original content
